Supports .csv, .xlsx and .json — everything stays in your browser.

Where do I get this file?
  • TikTok Studio (best): on desktop go to TikTok Studio → Analytics → Content and use Download data. You get a spreadsheet with views, likes, comments, shares and more per video.
  • Account data export: Settings → Account → Download your data, choose JSON. Upload user_data.json — it includes post dates, links, captions and likes (TikTok doesn't include views there).
  • Any spreadsheet: columns are detected by name, e.g. Title, Date, Views, Likes, Comments, Shares, Saves, Duration, Avg watch time, Completion rate, New followers. Values like 12.3K and 1.2M are fine.
